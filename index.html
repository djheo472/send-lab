Small-scale engineering.
Real-world possibilities.

We study how mechanical energy and nanoscale interactions can be translated into useful electrical power and sensing signals.

SEND lab connects energy harvesting, self-powered devices and nanobiosensing through mechanical design and micro/nanotechnology. Led by Prof. Deokjae Heo, we are based in the School of Mechanical Engineering at Kumoh National Institute of Technology.

기계적 움직임을 전기에너지로, 미세한 변화를 유용한 신호로. SEND lab은 에너지 하베스팅, 자가발전 센서, 나노바이오센싱을 연결하는 소자와 시스템을 연구합니다.
